Control your NRGkick charging cable straight from Homey, over your own network and without the cloud. See at a glance whether the car is connected or charging, how much power it draws and how much energy each charge added, and get told when the NRGkick reports a fault or a raised temperature. Pause or resume charging, set the charging current, an energy limit or the number of phases from the device or from a Flow, and let Homey Energy steer the charging power, for example to charge on solar surplus.

Works with the NRGkick (second generation, WiFi) with SmartModule firmware 4.0.0.0 or newer. Experimental support for the first generation: through the NRGkick Connect module (reading and control), or directly over Bluetooth. The first-generation support is not yet tested on real devices; feedback from owners is welcome. Bluetooth (first generation): limited and untested. It only reads values for now, it cannot control charging yet. Bluetooth has a short range: Homey must be close to the NRGkick, typically within about 10 metres and ideally without walls in between. While the NRGkick app on your phone is connected, Homey cannot read the NRGkick. This is an unofficial app, not affiliated with or endorsed by DiniTech GmbH.
